## Motivation and temporal boundary

The full historical compatibility screen contained no unused compatible
families. Snow and Urban content additionally required proprietary archives
absent from the authorized runtime. To avoid outcome-guided reuse or unlawful
asset acquisition, a new Temperate population was frozen from a public map

## Reproducible materialization and release boundary

Each source byte string is content-addressed by SHA-1. The preparation tool
rewrites only its local filename from `fresh_<sha1>.map` to
`method_v3_fresh_<sha1>.map` under the driver data directory; it does not
change map bytes. The committed catalog and target manifest bind SHA-256, byte
count, family identity, and repository-relative path. The role-blind target
population contains all 66 families and has commitment
`7a106dbb816dea419d63d428f6199604e2d7f50e85909c0337ff530880339bae`.

Third-party map bytes remain untracked. Public release includes the source
query, source/content commitments, exact metadata and hashes, and a retrieval
tool that downloads from the public content-addressed endpoint and refuses any
SHA-1, SHA-256, or byte-count mismatch. If redistribution permission is not
independently established, the release does not include map bytes.

## Prospective roles

Only families with a `pass` verdict in the complete fresh-profile screen enter
the role freezer. Review and failure verdicts are ineligible. No compatibility
failure may be replaced based on a StrongBot or Supalosa outcome.

Passing family IDs are ranked by

`SHA-256("chrono-divide-method-v3-role-v1\0" + family_id)`.

If at least 40 pass, assign in rank order:

1. 16 open training families;
2. four development-wave-A families;
3. four development-wave-B families;
4. 16 sealed confirmatory families; and
5. any remainder as ordered substitutes.

Role identities and exact map hashes are stored in private manifests. Public
artifacts expose only counts and commitments. Development and confirmatory
identities may not enter policy code, map profiles, coordinates, routes, or
target-priority rules.

If 24--39 families pass, no reduced design is automatically authorized. A new
prospective amendment must specify the reduced role allocation, uncertainty,
and claim scope before any policy outcome on a fresh family. Fewer than 24
passes blocks fresh development and confirmation.
