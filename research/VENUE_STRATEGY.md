## No-travel requirement

The official home page says speakers unable to travel may exceptionally
present remotely with hybrid support. The presentation page provides complete
live Zoom instructions for both oral and poster sessions. Remote presenters
must attend synchronously; a prerecorded video is backup only and does not
avoid the live no-show rule.

This offers a possible no-travel route, but it is not an unconditional remote
guarantee. Before submission or registration, obtain written instructions and
approval for the exception. If the secretariat will not confirm synchronous
remote presentation, do not submit to this venue; switch only to a venue with
a documented remote route.

## AI and double-blind ruling

ICAART requires AI-generated content to be disclosed and the system cited, but
its double-blind instructions also say acknowledgments must be omitted. The
review manuscript therefore uses an identity-neutral Generative AI Disclosure
and cites the official OpenAI Codex page. Ask the secretariat to approve that
placement or specify the portal field/anonymous wording; do not minimize the
recorded assistance as grammar correction.
