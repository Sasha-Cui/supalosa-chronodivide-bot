## Decision-critical objections

### 1. This is hard-coded map tuning.

Agree that StrongBot uses exact map/start predicates. The contribution is an
auditable map-profiled scripted agent, not a universal policy. Fresh balanced
replication, reciprocal slots, and inactive-cell equality establish the
declared scope; exact-coordinate generalization remains a limitation.

### 2. Only Supalosa is beaten.

Correct. Reliable superiority is established only against pinned Supalosa on
HFO and Peak. The independent Advanced reversal is reported prominently and
prevents a general bot-ranking claim.

### 3. This is not machine learning or a novel optimizer.

Correct. StrongBot is a deterministic layered scripted agent. The original
work is the agent mechanisms, prospective isolation/replication process, and
literal-objective evidence contract. The paper explicitly disclaims general
optimizer novelty.

### 4. The Advanced failure undermines the positive result.

It limits external validity but does not invalidate the frozen Supalosa
estimands. Both policies were paired on the same Advanced cases; the reversal
is evidence of opponent-specific overfitting and motivates multi-opponent
training.

### 5. A nominal win could be caused by resignation.

Both bots' resignation calls are suppressed symmetrically. A win requires
literal removal of every enemy building. Nonliteral engine termination is a
draw.

### 6. Two maps are not broad generalization.

Correct. HFO covers four starts and Peak two, with nine countries and both
slots, but the map population is still narrow. The paper claims two-map
reliability, not broad map generalization.

### 7. The mechanism claims are post-hoc stories.

Each final mechanism has a prospectively frozen paired replication and a
separate outcome-free activation/isolation gate. These support local
implemented-mechanism claims, not complete causal decomposition of the
inherited bot.

### 8. Screenshots are cherry-picked.

Categories, eligibility, hash-minimum selection, and updates were frozen before
rendering. Renderer-on/off trajectories and endpoints match. Screenshots remain
illustrations; aggregate replications carry the inference.

### 9. Hundreds of games are treated as independent.

Pooled Wilson bounds summarize literal wins. HFO also equal-weights 36
country-start cells; Peak reports a paired case-level t lower bound and an
18-cell country-start lower bound. The manuscript names each unit.

### 10. Third-party rights prevent reproducibility.

The anonymous artifact regenerates every reported number and plot from compact
aggregate evidence and includes verified frames. It excludes unlicensed bot
trees, runtimes, maps, and assets. Full gameplay reproduction requires
rights-holder acquisition guidance and is not overstated.
