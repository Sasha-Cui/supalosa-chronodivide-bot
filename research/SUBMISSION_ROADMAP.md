# ICAART 2027 submission roadmap

Updated: **2026-08-30**

Operational target: ICAART 2027 first-stage regular paper, deadline
**2026-09-15 AOE**. The technical paper and anonymous artifact are frozen; no
new gameplay is planned.

## 30 August--2 September: policy and human verification

- Send the ICAART secretariat inquiry in `CONTACT_TEMPLATES.md` covering the
  remote exception procedure, AI disclosure placement, public repository, and
  reviewer artifact.
- Give the 12-page PDF to one technically literate cold reader without coaching.
- Have the human author verify the abstract, every primary number, all cited
  sources, policy pseudocode, captions, limitations, and disclosure.
- Prepare author order, affiliations, ORCIDs, funding, conflicts, and contact
  data in a private record.
- Send the Supalosa and Chrono Divide permission/citation inquiries now that
  the paper is ready.

Exit: written venue questions sent, human claim/citation sign-off complete, and
cold-reader comments recorded.

## 3--6 September: claim-preserving revision

- Fix only demonstrated clarity, citation, or formatting problems.
- Do not add gameplay, inspect unused sealed populations, weaken gates, or
  remove the Advanced result.
- Rebuild `make -C paper_scitepress submission-check` after any edit.
- Rebuild the anonymous archive twice and update its frozen identity after any
  included-file change.
- Render and inspect every changed PDF page.

Exit: no unresolved reader issue and all hashes/checks synchronized.

## 7--10 September: upload rehearsal

- Create the PRIMORIS entry and select Regular Paper, Agents, Agent Models and
  Architectures, Simulation, and Task Planning and Execution when available.
- Paste only `build/submission_metadata.json` values into portal fields.
- Confirm whether the 1.32 MB anonymous tar.gz can be attached; otherwise omit
  it from review and keep the paper self-contained.
- Confirm remote-presentation procedure in writing before making a
  nonrefundable registration decision.
- Keep author-owned public materials from advertising or linking the submitted
  PDF during the double-blind period.

Exit: successful dry run with no anonymity leak and a documented remote route.

## 11--15 September: final submission

- Reverify deadline and AOE timezone on the official Important Dates page.
- Run the full test suite, SCITEPRESS submission check, frozen-archive verifier,
  and final anonymity scan.
- Compare final PDF and archive hashes with `SCITEPRESS_QA.md` and
  `FROZEN_IDENTITY.json`.
- Upload before the last day when practical.
- Download the venue copies and compare hashes, page count, rendering, title,
  abstract, keywords, and anonymous author block.
- Store submission ID, confirmation email, exact uploads, and hashes privately.

Exit: verified submission no later than **2026-09-15 AOE**.

## If September is blocked

- Use the **2026-10-22 AOE** second regular stage if the remote/AI/anonymity
  answers become workable.
- Consider the **2026-12-03 AOE** workshop route only after a suitable workshop
  and remote-presentation rule are officially posted.
- Otherwise use the no-travel journal fallback rather than promising physical
  attendance.

Never submit the same work simultaneously to incompatible archival venues.
