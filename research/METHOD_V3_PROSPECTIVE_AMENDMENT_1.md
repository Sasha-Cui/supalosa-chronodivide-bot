Status: **frozen before the first method-v3 outcome-bearing launch**.

Frozen on: **2026-08-11**.

This amendment changes only the map population available to method-v3
development. It does not alter the actual-win endpoint, the one-policy
all-country target, the fresh-development gates, the confirmatory gates, or the
claim boundary in `METHOD_V3_PROSPECTIVE_PROGRAM.md`.

representatives under authoritative Slurm account `pi_jss233`. The job and its

## Authorized development population

Method-v3 Stages 1 and 2 may proceed on the 22 compatible families in the
original private `train` role. Those families and their prior outcomes are open
development material. Their existing public/private commitment chain, exact
representative bytes, and family identities remain unchanged.

The amendment does **not** authorize any family from a prior development,
reserve, test, review, or fail role for method-v3 Stage 1 or Stage 2. It also
does not authorize synthetic variants, duplicate revisions, or outcome-guided
map selection.

Every Stage-1 arm must receive the identical schedule:

- all 22 opened training families;
- all nine same-country mirrors;
- both reciprocal candidate slots;
- one prospectively derived engine seed per family-country block; and
- an 18,000-tick cap.

A family-country block is indivisible and contains both slots for every frozen
arm. Outcome-bearing games have no retry. Technical incompletion blocks
analysis of the entire campaign until its launch accounting is reconciled.

## Actual-win invariant

A candidate win is accepted only when the engine marks Supalosa defeated under
`shortGame=true` and Supalosa's terminal building count is zero. A baseline win
is accepted only under the reciprocal condition. Any mismatch is a technical
failure. An unfinished tick-cap game is a draw regardless of credits, army,
combatant lead, or terminal building advantage.

## Remaining hard block

Stages 3 and confirmatory evaluation remain prohibited until at least 24 fresh
compatible families exist for the two four-family development waves and the
16-family confirmatory set; the original 40-family design remains preferred.
Adding legally obtained Snow/Urban archives, or freezing a genuinely new and
deduplicated Temperate source population, requires another prospective,
outcome-free amendment and a complete compatibility screen before role
assignment.

No Stage-1 or Stage-2 result can itself support a paper claim that StrongBot
reliably beats Supalosa. These opened-family results may select and improve a
policy only.
