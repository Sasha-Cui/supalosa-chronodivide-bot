## Prospective policy-v5 correction

Policy v5 replaces unconditional preemptive corridor clearing with a completion
race computed only from public complete state:

1. Estimate building-completion ticks as the nearest selected attacker's
   approach time plus target hit points divided by aggregate compatible
   anti-building damage per tick.
2. Identify damage-capable enemy route threats using the existing corridor and
   mutual-damage checks.
3. Estimate detachment-survival ticks as aggregate selected hit points divided
   by aggregate route-threat damage per tick against the selected force.
4. If the building is already in range, no route threat exists, or estimated
   building completion is no slower than estimated detachment destruction,
   attack the building.
5. Otherwise clear only the route threat with the largest removable threat
   score, defined prospectively as its damage rate against the detachment
   divided by its estimated hit points under the selected force's damage rate.
6. Recompute the race every objective decision and resume the same committed
   building as soon as the completion race favors it.

Undefined or zero threat damage yields infinite survival and therefore a
building attack. Undefined or zero building damage fails closed to the existing
predecessor path rather than authorizing a force sweep. Off-route forces remain
absent from the race regardless of count.

no-progress handling, maximum lease duration, and exact external Supalosa code
remain unchanged. Policy v5 uses a distinct exact schema and canonical hash.
