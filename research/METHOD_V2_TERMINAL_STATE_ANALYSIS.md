# Method-v2 terminal-state analysis

Status: **complete; descriptive evidence supports an army-production mechanism,
but terminal snapshots do not establish a causal trajectory**.

This post-outcome analysis explains the frozen method-v2 results without
launching, selecting, or rerunning any game. The deterministic aggregate is
`research/artifacts/method_v2_terminal_state_analysis_v1.json` (SHA-256
`61d84614a5f8088bb38f263a772ec1c34a1334283d51098e96af3d85839dc6b4`).

Every shard was complete and technically clean. Every completion matched its

## Confirmatory outcome decomposition

The champion's 0.53516 score versus the frozen generic reference policy's
0.19922 is an
improvement of 0.33594 on 256 paired games per method. In paired outcome terms:

- 28 reference losses became champion wins and 104 became tick-cap draws.
- 18 reference draws became champion wins.
- Six reference draws regressed to champion losses.
- One reference win and 99 other outcomes were unchanged at the score level.

Equivalently, 150 pairs improved, six regressed, and 100 had the same frozen
score. The champion gained 46 wins, 80 draws, and avoided 126 losses relative
to the reference. This makes the practical effect clearer: the policy primarily
turns rapid or eventual losses into survival and, less often, outright wins.
The absolute result still does **not** pass the frozen reliable-win gate against
Supalosa.

## Terminal-state pattern

Across all confirmatory pairs, the champion improves its terminal
candidate-minus-opponent balance by 30.58 combatants and 31.19 total units on
average, while holding 1,217.93 fewer credits. Outcome changes mechanically
affect many terminal snapshots, so the stable draw-to-draw stratum is the more
conservative descriptive comparison. In the 76 pairs that remain tick-cap
draws under both methods, game length and score are identical, but the champion
has a 22.71 larger relative combatant balance, a 12.43 larger relative unit
balance, and 683.82 fewer relative credits at the terminal tick.

This is consistent with a policy that converts banked resources and economic
capacity into fielded combat power. It is not proof of when, how, or why that
conversion occurred: the preserved evidence has no action trace, production
timeline, combat log, or intermediate state trajectory.

## Diagnostic triangulation

The common-seed optimizer diagnostic shows the same qualitative pattern. The
champion's terminal candidate-minus-opponent combatant balance is 23.88,
compared with 8.66 for the equal average of the five independently selected
local optimizer policies. Its corresponding unit balances are 23.79 and 8.38.
This agrees with the positive 0.08250 score estimate, but that score interval
includes zero and remains suggestive.

The policy-component diagnostic localizes the strongest observed change to the
joint `attackCompositionPolicy` + `strategicPlan` group. Reverting that group
from infantry+rush to assault+macro lowers score by 0.33125 and changes 43
losses into 33 draws and 10 wins, while causing five score regressions. The
champion's relative terminal balance is 19.89 combatants and 15.70 units higher
than this revert overall, with 857.68 fewer credits. Even among the 21 pairs
that remain draw-to-draw, the differences are +17.71 combatants, +2.86 units,
and -394.14 credits. The prespecified familywise interval for this component
contrast nevertheless includes zero, so the component is the dominant
observed explanation rather than an established multiplicity-controlled
effect.

Champion and `revertScouting` are exactly equal in all 80 paired games across
map identity, all recorded seeds, starts, ticks, outcome fields, and complete
candidate and opponent terminal states (including unit/building counts by
name). This shows no detected endpoint effect of the scouting-setting change
on this panel. It does not prove identical internal actions because those were
not logged.

## Paper claim boundary

The defensible interpretation is that method-v2 optimization found a policy
that robustly improves the frozen generic StrongBot reference, largely by
replacing losses with survival or wins and by ending matched games with substantially
more combat power and fewer banked credits. The strategy group is the principal
observed component signal. The evidence does not establish a novel learning
algorithm, a multiplicity-controlled single-component cause, or a reliable
absolute win rate above 0.5 against Supalosa.

The reference is `DEFAULT_RESEARCH_POLICY`, not the fork's map-profile-enabled
deployed constructor default; frozen result fields retain the label `default`.

No further outcome-bearing game is authorized by this analysis. New
instrumented games could study trajectories in future work, but they would be
post-confirmatory exploratory evidence and are not necessary to close the
current empirical program.
