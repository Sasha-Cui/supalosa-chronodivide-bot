# Method-v5 prospective literal-win program

Status: frozen before any Method-v5 game. This protocol operationalizes the
external prospective program and endpoint-v4 evidence under versioned source.

## Claim boundary

Method-v5 asks whether a generic, visibility-limited closeout controller added
to the exact pinned external Supalosa `DefaultStrategy` can obtain more literal
wins than that baseline. A literal win is the opponent-attributed physical
destruction of every currently enemy-owned building. The winner need not retain
a building. Engine defeat, resignation, score, and cleanup are not substitutes.

Method-v4 and earlier outcomes use a different endpoint and are training
diagnostics only. Method-v5 open-training outcomes cannot support a paper claim.

## Policy information boundary

The candidate may use own objects, currently visible enemy objects, remembered
sightings, public map geometry and starts, visibility queries, production
availability, and ordinary Supalosa state. Only the evaluator may call
`getAllUnits`. Evaluator ledgers cannot enter policy decisions or telemetry.

## Frozen first screen

- 22 permanently open training families inherited by exact byte commitment;
- all nine countries, candidate and baseline using the same country;
- both physical slots under one common seed per family-country block;
- eight frozen arms: exact baseline control, memory/search, four-unit adaptive
  air capability, early activation, rapid three-tick closeout refresh, a
  two-unit reserve, higher production priority, and one prospective aggressive
  arm combining the latter four settings;
- 3,168 total games, no retry, 24,000-tick cap, `shortGame=false`, and symmetric
  suppression and counting of resignation attempts; and
- Slurm account `pi_jss233` only.

The full population must complete cleanly before any outcome ranking. A partial
campaign, endpoint-less engine finish, unauthorized job, duplicated launch,
missing reciprocal slot, or policy information leak blocks analysis.

## Advancement

A policy advances only if its literal win probability is strictly above 0.50,
its wins exceed losses, it has wins exceeding losses in at least seven of nine
countries, and all 3,168 launches pass the technical gate. Ranking uses wins,
win-loss margin, draw rate, country stability, time to literal win, and canonical
policy hash in that order.

If no arm advances, diagnose only the prespecified open-training failure classes
(activation, target discovery, attacker compatibility, reachability, production,
order churn, home collapse, and cap after progress), freeze a new version, and
run a complete fresh all-country screen. Fresh development and confirmatory
outcomes remain sealed during refinement.

## Fresh-map sequence

After an open-training policy advances:

1. run the outcome-blind passive compatibility gate on all 56 prospectively
   frozen fresh families under the same current gate;
2. freeze development-A, development-B, and confirmatory roles without policy
   outcome access;
3. require development-A and development-B positive gates before confirmation;
4. run one sealed confirmatory population with family-cluster uncertainty; and
5. run frozen ablations and deterministic screenshot selection only after the
   primary confirmatory result is complete.

No paper conclusion is written before the relevant results and uncertainty are
complete.
