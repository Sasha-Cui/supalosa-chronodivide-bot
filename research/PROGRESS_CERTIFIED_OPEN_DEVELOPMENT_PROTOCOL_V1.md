# Progress-certified objective conversion open-development protocol, version 1

Status: **prospective; frozen before outcome-bearing execution**

## Research question

Can a map-independent objective overlay on the exact pinned external Supalosa
core convert more Chrono Divide games into literal wins by treating enemy forces
as instrumental route/interception blockers, ranking complete
blocker-then-building missions, releasing the final-building reserve, and
abandoning missions that produce no physical damage?

The primary endpoint is the probability of a literal candidate win: every
enemy building is physically destroyed and the endpoint ledger attributes the
decisive destruction to the candidate. A favorable state at tick 24,000,
engine resignation, sale, capture, nonliteral termination, or draw-adjusted
score does not count as a win.

## Evidentiary boundary

This is open development, not a held-out paper evaluation. It reuses all ten
families from the completed continuous-offense campaign without filtering any
family. Their prior outcomes are open and influenced the new mechanism, so no
old or new game on these families can support a confirmatory performance claim.
No prior game, seed, or result is pooled with this campaign.

The prior continuous-offense campaign is complete null evidence. It showed that
the frozen macro core was below exact external self-control, fixed target-class
preferences did not improve literal win probability, blocker commands could
repeat without physical progress, activation was often late, and two
combatants remained reserved in every observed exact final-building snapshot.

## Fixed method boundary

- Candidate core: exact pinned external Supalosa production, scouting, defense,
  and ordinary combat strategy.
- Opponent: a separately instantiated copy of the same pinned external Supalosa
  package.
- Overlay information interface: declared `public_complete_state`; no endpoint,
  winner, score, disposition, or future-state access.
- Candidate and opponent use the same country and engine seed with reciprocal
  physical slots. Participant RNG identities remain distinct and recorded.
- Default map profiles and exact-map tactics remain outside the candidate.
- Every launch and failure counts. There are no start filters, outcome filters,
  outcome-dependent retries, or selective reruns.

## Frozen causal arms

The exact arm order, schemas, policy fields, and hashes are generated by
`progressCertifiedExperimentPolicy.ts`:

1. `external_baseline_control`: disabled overlay and exact external candidate.
2. `external_final_building_direct`: when one building remains, use the full
   compatible force against it and ignore all enemy forces.
3. `external_final_building_hybrid`: when one building remains, attack it
   directly when survivable; otherwise clear route/interception blockers, with
   physical-progress deadlines.
4. `external_low_count_direct`: apply the building-only rule after the guarded
   transition to at most five enemy buildings.
5. `external_low_count_route_no_deadline`: use route blockers after the same
   transition but set both no-damage deadlines above the 24,000-tick episode
   cap. This is the liveness ablation.
6. `external_low_count_progress_hybrid`: proposed complete method: guarded
   low-building activation, route/interception blockers, complete-mission
   ranking, zero final reserve, a 360-tick blocker no-damage deadline, and a
   600-tick building no-damage deadline.

The final-building arms activate no earlier than tick 3,600 and only at exact
building count one. The low-count arms may activate on an observed transition
from above five to at most five after tick 3,600, or at tick 7,200 if already in
scope. All enabled arms use two ordinary reserve combatants and zero terminal
reserve combatants.

## Technical gate

Do not summarize any outcome until all 90 shards complete and one fail-closed
gate reconciles:

- 90 unique plans, source/runtime/baseline/map commitments, scheduler task IDs,
  manifests, event ledgers, and summaries;
- exactly 1,080 counted launches and 1,080 valid completions;
- both slots for every family-country-arm block;
- scheduler account `pi_jss233` and exit `0:0` for every task;
- zero source, runtime, seed, map, policy, endpoint, participant, or information
  boundary violations;
- zero forwarded resignations or nonliteral credited wins; and
- enabled intervention exposure for every country, with nonexposure of a
  final-only arm reported descriptively rather than repaired selectively.

A code or protocol defect invalidates the complete campaign. Repair under a new
version and fresh seeds; never rerun only failed or favorable games.

## Scheduled analysis and development gate

After the complete technical gate, perform one scheduled full-population
analysis. Rank enabled arms by, in order: the weaker Allied/Soviet literal-win
probability, family-macro literal-win probability, win-minus-loss rate, number
of countries with wins exceeding losses, lower draw probability, faster median
literal-win tick, and canonical policy ID.

For the selected enabled arm, let the ten family-level literal-win proportions
be $p_1,\ldots,p_{10}$, with mean $\bar p$ and sample standard deviation $s$.
The frozen one-sided 80% family-clustered lower bound is

$$
L_{0.80}=\bar p-t_{0.80,9}\frac{s}{\sqrt{10}},
\qquad t_{0.80,9}=0.8834038596855205.
$$

Advance only if every check passes:

1. $L_{0.80}>0.5$;
2. literal wins exceed losses overall;
3. pooled Allied and pooled Soviet literal-win probabilities each exceed 0.5;
4. wins exceed losses in at least seven of nine countries;
5. the family-macro paired literal-win effect over
   `external_baseline_control` is positive;
6. the family-macro draw-probability effect over that control is negative; and
7. every technical and information-boundary gate passes.

This permissive selected-arm screen is allowed to guide a later fresh
development or confirmatory method freeze. It is not corrected for arm
selection and therefore cannot be reported as held-out evidence.

## Prespecified diagnostics

After the scheduled unblinding, report complete-population diagnostics for all
arms: W/D/L, literal-win probability, win time, family/faction/country strata,
paired effects over control, activation count and time, building and blocker
decisions, physical-progress intervals, deadline expirations, stale fallback,
target switches, reserve release, assigned-force fraction, and terminal enemy
building count. Diagnostics explain failure modes but cannot replace the
primary gate or justify selective replay.

If no arm advances, use these open diagnostics to define a genuinely new method
version and new seeds. Do not write a positive paper narrative. If an arm
advances, freeze it before any new-family evaluation and retain the current
population only as development evidence.
