## Research question

Can a fixed, map-independent Chrono Divide agent reliably defeat the pinned
external Supalosa bot by combining the previously trained macro champion with a
continuous objective scheduler that destroys enemy forces only when they block
the route to physical elimination of all enemy buildings?

The primary endpoint is literal opponent-attributed destruction of every enemy
building. Engine resignation, sale, capture, engine victory flags, favorable
state at the tick cap, and nonliteral stalemate termination are not wins.

## Information and implementation boundaries

- Opponent: the clean pinned external Supalosa package and runtime recorded in
  each manifest.
- Candidate code: main branch only, with default map profiles and exact-map
  tactics disabled.
- Macro policy: frozen training-selected policy
  `ab0071009cbd5f1fc9e2f1a76a562455a0ebeacf77dac1cd9c13b9fc6374203f`.
- Scheduler information interface: declared `public_complete_state`; it may
  enumerate public live enemy units but receives no endpoint adjudicator,
  dispositions, winner, score, or future state.
- Candidate and baseline share each engine seed and country, with reciprocal
  physical slots. Participant RNG seeds are identity-specific and recorded.
- Every attempted game is counted. There are no start filters, outcome filters,
  retries, or selective reruns.

## Frozen causal arms

The exact arm order and canonical hashes are generated by
`continuousOffenseExperimentPolicy.ts`. The policy definitions are unchanged
from version 1; version 2 changes only the prospective runtime guard, gate
evidence, and fresh engine seeds:

1. `external_baseline_control`: exact external Supalosa versus itself.
2. `macro_champion_control`: frozen trained macro champion without the new
   scheduler.
3. `macro_all_forces_first`: macro champion plus a scheduler that attacks every
   observed enemy armed force before buildings.
4. `macro_buildings_only`: macro champion plus a scheduler that never diverts
   for enemy forces.
5. `macro_route_blockers_minimum`: route-blocker policy with a minimum sufficient
   compatible strike detachment.
6. `macro_route_blockers_full`: proposed policy; route blockers only, with the
   full compatible forward force and the two combatants nearest home reserved.

All scheduler arms share activation, search, route geometry, calibration,
reserve, liveness, and ordering parameters. The proposed scheduler takes over
after a guarded public transition from more than five to at most five enemy
buildings after tick 7,200, or at fixed tick 12,600. This preserves the learned
rush opening while making late offensive liveness mandatory.

## Selection and development advancement rule

Rank the four enabled scheduler arms using, in order:

1. larger minimum literal-win probability across Allied and Soviet factions;
2. larger equally family-weighted literal-win probability;
3. larger pooled win-minus-loss rate;
4. more countries with wins exceeding losses;
5. lower draw probability;
6. earlier median literal-win tick; and
7. ascending canonical policy SHA-256.

The selected arm advances only if every condition holds:

- its one-sided family-clustered 80% lower bound for literal-win probability is
  above 0.50 using ten family means and Student's $t_9$ critical value
  `0.8834038596855205`;
- literal wins exceed literal losses overall;
- Allied and Soviet pooled literal-win probabilities are each above 0.50;
- wins exceed losses in at least seven of nine countries;
- its equally family-weighted paired literal-win effect is positive over both
  `external_baseline_control` and `macro_champion_control`;
- it reduces the equally family-weighted draw probability relative to the macro
  champion control;
- all technical and intervention-exposure checks pass.

The macro champion control is reported separately to decompose improvement from
training versus closeout. The fight-all, buildings-only, and minimum-detachment
arms are mechanistic ablations, not alternative post hoc rescue endpoints.

## Mechanism diagnostics

After the one scheduled complete-campaign unblinding, report by arm, family,
country, and faction:

- literal wins, draws, losses, and time to literal win;
- nonliteral engine termination versus tick-cap draws;
- activation mode and time;
- building-strike, terminal-strike, blocker-clear, search, and regroup counts;
- eligible, certified, assigned, and reserved combatants;
- assigned fraction and selected unit types;
- reserved and delegated idle/moving/attacking state;
- time since building damage and target commitment changes;
- terminal building counts; and
- physical building-destruction disposition categories.

These diagnostics explain the primary result; they cannot replace or redefine
the advancement rule.

## After open development

If the gate passes, freeze exactly one selected policy and move it without
further tuning to fresh development under a new protocol, then to a single
sealed confirmatory evaluation only if the fresh positive signal and technical
gates pass. If the gate fails, use only these permanently open aggregate
diagnostics to formulate a new method version. Do not inspect sealed families or
write a positive paper claim.
