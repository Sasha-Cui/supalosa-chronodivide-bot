# Fresh dual-endpoint retained-metadata seed audit scope v1

This operationalizes the existing seed-reservation prerequisite before any
new selector initialization. It does not change cases, seeds or scientific
gates. The audit itself creates no games.

## Inputs and scan boundaries

Audit the exact 1084 planned distinct seeds AND the entire proposed unsigned
interval [3765000000,3770000000), including signed-int32 equivalents.

Retained-metadata roots are project research-evidence, checkout research/artifacts,
and root/driver benchmark-results. Only filenames identifying manifests,
selection/selected cases, reservations, schedules, configurations, campaign
and allocation descriptors, inventories/registries/populations, plans,
parameters or seeds are eligible text metadata; standalone .seed files are
included. Eligible formats: JSON/JSONL/NDJSON, YAML, TOML, text, manifest and
CSV/TSV. Raw case/cell/result/outcome/summary/metric/trace files, generic event
logs, credentials, archives, binaries and replay streams are not opened.
Filename classification and skipped-extension/reason counts must be retained.

Also scan prospective protocol Markdown and source declarations under
research/runtime, research/scripts, research/slurm and driver src/training,
excluding tests and exact current-study source files. Source is read as
declarations, never executed. Record hashes and a seed/range inventory.

Read eligible bytes only for hashing and integer/range recognition; do not
interpret, retain or print W/D/L, scores or other competitive content.
Recognize decimal, underscore-grouped, comma-grouped and hexadecimal integer
literals, signed equivalents, and explicit reserved/seed interval arrays or
minimum/maximum range objects. Record numeric matches and byte offsets, not
surrounding file content.

Do not follow symlinks or enter .git, dependency, environment, runtime-asset
or cache trees. Do not access off-tree archives or private locations. Exclude
the new study output subtree and enumerate the exact current-study source/
protocol exclusions so the audit does not flag its own reservation.

## Declared-range review

Source review already identified the colliding 3300M method-v3 optimizer and
its 10M-run/1M-stage strides. That proposal is abandoned. Nearby producers
use 3690M plus a sequential family/country/slot index; 3730M plus 19*9 shard
indices; 3740M and 3745M plus the 9*2 country/slot index; and the next
terminal-objective campaign begins at 3770M plus nonnegative indices.
Record the reviewed source hashes and these derivations in the audit.

Actual metadata and explicit reserved ranges remain authoritative. The review
is not a static proof for every hypothetical caller or arbitrary future input.
Any concrete overlap, conflicting interval, unreadable eligible metadata or
input mutation fails closed. An unbounded or ambiguous declaration plausibly
covering the proposed range must be resolved before accepting the audit.

## Completeness, limits and execution

Hash every selected file, compare inode/size/mtime before and after, retain the
entire selected-path inventory, and record every read error. Store all audit
outputs immutably under the fresh study root. Require clean synchronized main,
exact program/scope/plan/source hashes, and actual Slurm pi_jss233 day accounting.
Use one CPU, 4 GiB and a two-hour limit; no GPU or simulation.

Passing means no collision within the declared retained-metadata/declaration
scope. It does not prove absence across excluded outcome files, archives,
unrecorded ad-hoc runs, arbitrary encodings or all historical bytes. Do not
describe it as universal freshness. A collision blocks initialization and
requires a recorded pre-outcome amendment, not silent reseeding.
