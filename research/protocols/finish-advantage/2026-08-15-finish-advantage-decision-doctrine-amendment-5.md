## Author clarification

Stalemate should not be a normal outcome of a policy that continually attacks
toward the literal win condition. The policy must keep eliminating enemy
buildings and eliminate armed forces only to the extent needed to make or
protect that progress.

The controller must decide dynamically whether the next useful target is a
building or a hostile force. When hostile forces are gone, the remaining
buildings are free terminal objectives and must be swept. When only one enemy
building remains alongside a large army, the army must be bypassed whenever
the building can be destroyed before that army prevents the strike or wins the
base race. Thus one building plus one hundred off-route tanks remains a direct
building strike, not a tank battle.

## Frozen priority hierarchy

At each eligible control interval:

1. Enumerate reachable building strikes using only the frozen public-state
   interface.
2. Prefer the strike with the shortest credible completion time when it beats
   both the causal-interception deadline and the friendly-base-loss deadline,
   subject to the frozen safety margin.
3. If no direct strike is safe, engage only the minimum identifiable hostile
   force responsible for the failed inequality. Recompute immediately after
   its removal, displacement, or loss of causal relevance.
4. Prioritize a production building when destroying it both advances the
   endpoint and prevents the regeneration or deployment of new blockers.
5. If the opponent has no meaningful resistance or replenishment path, commit
   all eligible unprotected attackers to continuous building cleanup.
6. If the current order produces no physical or approach progress within the
   frozen deadline, deterministically replan without globally sweeping forces.

Enemy-force count and enemy-force value are not independent reasons to attack
forces. A force becomes a target through its causal effect on the credible
time-to-win calculation.

## Composition invariant

The final-building V5 layer remains outside the multi-building finish layer.
The composite must prove that the V5 layer receives and retains authority when
one building remains, including the one-building-plus-many-off-route-forces
case. The multi-building layer must preserve protected Supalosa mission
ownership and remain action-free outside its frozen scope.

## Required deterministic evidence

Before any prospective outcome-bearing screen, executable tests or an
outcome-blind compatibility gate must establish:

- direct final-building priority over an arbitrarily large off-route army;
- minimum-blocker selection when one force can actually stop the strike;
- immediate building refocus after blocker destruction;
- base-race intervention only when the enemy wins the frozen timing inequality;
- continuous multi-building sweep after resistance disappears;
- production-building priority when it prevents renewed resistance;
- no-progress recovery without global force hunting; and
- preservation of protected Supalosa missions and the final-building handoff.

## Empirical interpretation

Draw reduction is successful only when it produces more literal wins without
an offsetting increase in losses. Mechanism activity or faster building damage
is explanatory evidence, not a substitute for complete paired win evidence.
Any further repair after an open screen must be prospectively frozen from the
authorized aggregate failure class; this amendment does not authorize tuning
from sealed games.

## Non-retroactivity

This amendment does not change the sealed V5 policy, its active jobs, its
technical gate, or its authorized unblinder. It applies only to later staged
implementation, outcome-blind compatibility checks, and newly frozen
prospective campaigns.
