# Finish-Advantage Confirmatory Design Requirements V1

Date: 2026-08-15

Status: prospective requirements record written before sealed V5 unblinding, before any finish-advantage competitive launch, and before any final-candidate or fresh-family outcome is known. This is not the executable confirmation protocol; its purpose is to prevent the later protocol from weakening the project objective after seeing development results.

## Claim that must be earned

The paper may claim that the final StrongBot reliably beats the exact pinned Supalosa bot only if a single, fresh-family, all-country, reciprocal-slot confirmation passes every primary requirement below. Improvement in draw-adjusted score alone is insufficient. Fewer draws without more literal wins is insufficient. Improvement over V5 without absolute superiority to Supalosa is insufficient.

The literal game endpoint is physical destruction of every enemy building. Suppressed resignation attempts, material advantage, remaining-force counts, evaluator labels, and nonliteral engine termination cannot define a win.

## Frozen comparison structure

The final confirmation must contain these three arms on identical family-country-slot-seed blocks:

1. `external_supalosa_control`: exact pinned external Supalosa as the candidate-side bot;
2. `visibility_aware_final_building_v5`: the unchanged V5 predecessor; and
3. one finish-advantage policy selected by the complete open causal screen and frozen without further outcome-dependent tuning.

Every arm must use the same exact external Supalosa opponent, endpoint, horizon, game settings, map bytes, participant-seed derivation, and engine seed within a block. Candidate slots 0 and 1 must be reciprocal starts under the same engine seed. The study must cover USA, Korea, France, Germany, Great Britain, Libya, Iraq, Cuba, and Russia with one shared coordinate-free policy.

## Fresh-family admission

A family is eligible only if a pre-outcome audit proves all of the following:

- its content identity is distinct from every training, development, diagnostic, V5-confirmatory, and binding no-reuse family;
- aliases, mode variants, and high-similarity duplicates collapse to one family before sampling;
- exact bytes, provenance, redistribution status, theater, bounds, and declared starts are recorded;
- static and live engine-compatibility gates pass under the pinned runtime;
- every prespecified country, slot, and engine-seed block is technically admissible; and
- no competitive outcome from that family has been generated or inspected before the final campaign freeze.

Technical compatibility observations are not policy outcomes and may determine eligibility only through a frozen outcome-blind rule. Failed or excluded families and their reasons remain in the released metadata.

## Primary estimands

Let `W`, `D`, and `L` denote candidate-oriented literal outcomes coded as win, draw, and loss. For each admitted map family, first average over all nine countries, both reciprocal slots, and every prospectively selected engine-seed block. Families are the independent inferential clusters and receive equal outer weight.

The executable protocol must test this conjunctive family of primary requirements:

1. **Literal-win superiority over exact Supalosa:** the one-sided family-clustered 95% lower confidence bound for the paired difference in literal-win probability between the final candidate and `external_supalosa_control` is strictly above zero.
2. **Absolute reliable superiority:** the one-sided family-clustered 95% lower confidence bound for the final candidate's absolute score margin above 0.5 is strictly above zero. Since score is `W + 0.5D`, this is equivalent to a strictly positive lower bound for one half of the win-probability-minus-loss-probability margin.
3. **Incremental literal-win improvement over V5:** the one-sided family-clustered 95% lower confidence bound for the paired difference in literal-win probability between the final candidate and V5 is strictly above zero.

These are intersection requirements: the project advances only when all three pass. Because success requires rejection of every component null, the final protocol should implement them as an intersection-union gate rather than selecting whichever endpoint is favorable.

The exact estimator, finite-cluster correction, critical constants, treatment of zero variance, and non-finite fail-closed behavior must be executable and hash-frozen after the final outcome-blind family count and seed-block count are known but before campaign launch.

## Required supporting gates

All of the following must also hold; none can rescue a failed primary requirement:

- the final candidate has strictly more total literal wins and strictly fewer draws than both comparators;
- no V5 paired win becomes a candidate draw or loss;
- the paired score effect is positive for both Allied and Soviet factions and for both reciprocal candidate slots;
- country-macro effects are reported for all nine countries, with a prospectively frozen broad-support rule and no subgroup rescue;
- leave-one-family-out effects for both primary paired comparisons remain positive;
- technical failures, timeouts, exclusions, warnings, launch counts, and arm imbalance satisfy a frozen complete-block rule; and
- every result resolves to exact code, configuration, map, seed, opponent, runtime, scheduler account, Slurm job ID, and raw-artifact commitments.

The final paper must report all nine country estimates and all family effects, including negative or null values. “All-country” means the same policy was prospectively evaluated in every supported country, not that post hoc favorable countries were selected.

## Power and sample-size gate

After the open screen selects one policy and the outcome-blind family audit fixes the eligible family count, use only development-family variance components and conservative upper uncertainty bounds to choose the number of fresh families and engine-seed blocks. Keep the smallest design whose prospective Monte Carlo 95% lower bound on unconditional power is at least 0.80 for a literal-win improvement of 0.05, whose invalid-analysis rate is below 0.05, and whose absolute-superiority and V5-increment gates are simultaneously estimable.

Do not reduce sample size because the selected development mean is large. If the admissible fresh-family population cannot support the frozen power gate, the confirmation is a no-go until the population is expanded outcome-blindly.

## Failure, rerun, and interpretation rules

- No partial or provisional outcome may be inspected.
- A technical defect invalidates the complete affected campaign version; no favorable or unfavorable cell may be rerun selectively.
- A policy repair after open-screen unblinding requires a new method version, prospective record, and fresh development seeds before another selector campaign.
- A failed confirmation cannot be rescued by another seed, country subset, theater subset, score endpoint, or mechanism subgroup on the same opened families.
- If any primary requirement fails, this project does not yet satisfy the author's requested “reliably beats Supalosa” paper objective and paper writing remains paused.

## Mechanism, uncertainty, and visual evidence

The final aggregate must preserve wins/draws/losses, transition tables, family/country/faction/slot effects, family-clustered intervals, sensitivity to frozen cutoff and horizon rules, certificate activations and revocations, direct building strikes, causal blocker clears, production denial, base-race abstentions, stall recoveries, protected-unit invariants, time to first building damage, and physical building-destruction timing.

Annotated screenshots may be generated only after the complete authorized unblinding. Example selection must follow a deterministic, result-independent rule over predeclared mechanism categories: final-building bypass, causal blocker removal, helpless multi-building sweep, protected-defense surplus strike, unseen-to-visible handoff, and one liveness-recovery or failure case. Every frame must cite its campaign, family, country, slot, seed, configuration, job ID, tick, and replay/checkpoint hash.

## Non-retroactivity

This record changes no active V5 job, V5 gate, open-development arm, state-audit selection rule, or existing result. It constrains only the later fresh-family confirmation and the claims that may follow it.
