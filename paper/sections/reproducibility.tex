\section{Reproducibility, Limitations, and Release}
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{Artifact and execution chain}

A compact final evidence artifact verifies eleven immutable aggregates and
generates every numerical macro, table, and plot in this paper. It pins source,
protocol, program, runtime, map, opponent, selection, prior-stage, aggregate,
and screenshot-manifest hashes. The generator rejects unexpected hashes,
missing fields, or numerical drift. Detailed result documents map each
scientific result to exact Slurm job IDs and the single prespecified simulation account. Raw cells use atomic writes
and checksums; scheduler
accounting must match the manifest before an aggregate can be opened.

The \OutcomeGameCount{} claim-bearing games exclude outcome-blind selection,
technical traces, and compatibility replays. Negative Advanced studies remain
in the evidence tree, as do failed or retired protocols; they are not pooled
with later prospective restarts. Screenshot replay is likewise separate from
statistical evaluation. The renderer is passive, and its
\ReplayCount{} independent replay pairs produced identical trajectories and
endpoints with and without frame capture.

The review artifact will include author-written orchestration, protocols,
tests, source patches, aggregate results, generation code, paper source, and
the \FrameCount{} deterministic frames. Chrono Divide, original Red Alert 2
archives, maps, the Supalosa package, and RA2Web assets remain subject to their
respective rights. Because the StrongBot package derives from an upstream tree
that presently has no redistribution license, we will not claim that the whole
bot can be relicensed unilaterally. The artifact instead supplies acquisition,
commit, and byte-hash verification instructions; a fuller release requires
permission from the relevant authors and rights holders.

\subsection{Limitations}

\paragraph{Opponent scope.}
Reliable superiority is established against one pinned Supalosa version on two
maps. The independent Advanced result reverses direction, so the paper supports
map-profiled opponent-specific strength, not universal bot ranking. An adaptive
opponent detector was technically feasible, but no Advanced specialist passed
the frozen strength gates; deploying a selector without a competent second
expert would be cosmetic.

\paragraph{Map scope.}
The positive evidence covers HFO and Peak of Perfection. HFO has four physical
starts and Peak two; both are evaluated across nine countries and reciprocal
slots. This is materially broader than one map or Iraq-only play, but far from
the full map distribution. Exact-coordinate dispatch also requires explicit
profiles for unseen maps.

\paragraph{Agent class and observability.}
StrongBot is a deterministic layered scripted agent with access to the same
structured full game state available through the offline bot API\@. It is not a
learned policy and the experiments do not compare sample efficiency with
reinforcement learning or program synthesis. Some interventions are defined in
human-readable domain terms, so their contribution is engineering and
evaluation methodology rather than a new general optimizer.

\paragraph{Residual failures.}
Four HFO games hit the 90,000-update cap. The preserved example loses all
mobile force before three enemy buildings are eliminated. A broader policy
would need explicit force-reconstitution and reachability reasoning, not simply
more frequent attack orders. Peak also remains start- and side-dependent even
though every prespecified stratum is positive.

\paragraph{Causal resolution.}
Paired component replications and inactive-cell trace equality isolate the
declared mechanisms more strongly than aggregate win rates alone. They do not
identify every interaction inside the inherited economy and mission system.
The game frames are deterministic observations, not counterfactual causal
tests.

\paragraph{Research adaptation.}
The final study follows several complete negative development programs. Each
pivot was declared prospectively with fresh cases and sealed downstream
populations, but the overall project is not a single-shot preregistration.
Our defense is transparent lineage, preserved negative evidence, and
confirmation on data unavailable during selection.

\subsection{Generative AI disclosure}

OpenAI Codex~\cite{openai2026codex} was used under the author's direction to
inspect code, implement
and test research infrastructure, orchestrate prespecified cluster campaigns,
reduce immutable evidence, and assist with manuscript drafting and figure
assembly. The human author chose the research questions and protocols, reviewed
the code and empirical interpretations, and remains responsible for every
claim. No model-generated result, screenshot, citation, or numerical value was
accepted without verification against the preserved artifacts.
