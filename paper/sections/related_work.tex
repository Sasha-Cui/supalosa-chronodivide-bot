\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Configuring scripted RTS agents.}
Evolutionary optimization of hand-authored game-bot parameters is established
practice. Fern{\'a}ndez-Ares et al. optimized rule-system constants for Planet
Wars~\cite{fernandezAres2011optimizing}; Young and Hawes evolved StarCraft goal
priorities~\cite{young2012goal}; Othman et al. optimized XML-configured tactical
behavior~\cite{othman2012starcraft}; and Liu et al. evolved microbehavior
parameters and examined transfer to unseen combat scenarios
\cite{liu2016microbehaviors}. Ouessai et al. used a genetic algorithm to
configure parametric action-preselection heuristics for specific $\mu$RTS
map--opponent settings, then evaluated the resulting agent against several
agents on those maps~\cite{ouessai2022evolving}. Mora et al. showed
specifically that noisy RTS fitness requires repeated combats, multiple maps,
and incumbent
reevaluation~\cite{mora2012noisy}. Recent \emph{Tales of Tribute} work likewise
compares evolutionary training modes for a 20-weight bot
\cite{castejon2026tales}. These studies establish scripted-bot tuning and
mutation search; our distinction is family-disjoint held-out evaluation.

The closest conceptual precedents specialize by map: Mari{\~n}o et al.
synthesized map-specific programmatic $\mu$RTS strategies from a
domain-specific language~\cite{marino2021programmatic}, while
Fern{\'a}ndez-Ares et al. selected evolved policies from online map
characterization~\cite{fernandezAres2012map}. Later programmatic-strategy work
strengthened synthesis with learned sketches, bilevel feature--program search,
opponent-set selection, and semantic-space neighborhoods
\cite{medeiros2022sketches,aleixo2023bilevel,moraes2023opponents,moraes2024semantic}.
Our initial method followed map conditioning and failed its development gate.
The final paper instead
studies one generic policy, disabling exact-map profiles, coordinates,
orientation branches, and map signatures. We make no synthesis or optimizer
claim; the distinction is family-disjoint, provenance-bound evaluation.

\paragraph{Algorithm configuration.}
Sequential model-based algorithm configuration and irace formalize tuning
across problem-instance sets and separate tuning from unseen evaluation
instances~\cite{hutter2011smac,lopezIbanez2016irace}. The N-tuple bandit
evolutionary algorithm is particularly relevant to discrete game-agent
configuration~\cite{lucas2018ntbea}, and direct game-agent
comparisons have found model-based configurators competitive while exposing
parameter interactions~\cite{lucas2019model}. Hyperband allocates increasing
resources to a shrinking candidate set~\cite{li2018hyperband}; our fixed
successive-halving schedule uses the same general resource-allocation idea.
Best-practice reviews warn that configurator comparisons are easily distorted
by scenario construction, over-tuning, and unequal budgets
\cite{eggensperger2019pitfalls}. We therefore do not claim optimizer
superiority: the study includes no standard configurator or hand-tuning
baseline. The training algorithm is a fixed, inexpensive mechanism for
producing one policy whose evaluation can be audited.

\paragraph{Generalization and statistical evaluation.}
CoinRun and Procgen demonstrated that game agents can overfit even sizable
sets of levels and motivated explicit training--test distributions
\cite{cobbe2019coinrun,cobbe2020procgen}. The General Video Game AI learning
track likewise trains on visible levels and evaluates hidden ones; Balla et al.
show that the choice of training levels and environment stochasticity
materially affect generalization~\cite{balla2020generalisation}. Held-out level
evaluation is therefore established. Chrono Divide adds a different data
problem: its maps are not procedural independent draws, because exact copies
and revisions can coexist. We consequently split at a conservatively
constructed family level rather than at a filename level. The inferential unit
is also the family, not each correlated match.
Agarwal et al. showed how point estimates from a few tasks or runs can reverse
under uncertainty-aware analysis~\cite{agarwal2021precipice}. We use balanced
family estimands, family-clustered intervals, and diagnostic family bootstrap
and sign-flip analyses.

Protocol variation can change conclusions even within one standardized game
platform: Machado et al. audit divergent Arcade Learning Environment practices
and introduce controlled stochasticity~\cite{machado2018ale}, while Henderson
et al. document sensitivity to environment nondeterminism, random seeds, and
reporting choices in deep reinforcement learning~\cite{henderson2018matters}.
Our policies are scripted rather than learned this way, but these evaluation
threats transfer to stochastic head-to-head simulation. We therefore pin the
runtime and expose the simulator and participant random streams rather than
treating repeat count alone as reproducibility.

Shared seeds and reciprocal starts act as paired simulation controls. Common
random numbers are a classical variance-reduction design for comparing
policies under matched stochastic inputs~\cite{schruben2011crn}. In our
setting, an engine stream alone is insufficient because bot callbacks also
consume randomness. We therefore isolate participant-specific streams so an
extra draw by StrongBot cannot shift Supalosa's randomness. The resulting
contribution is an integrated evaluation protocol for a real scripted RTS
codebase: deterministic pairing, baseline isolation, family leakage control,
complete scheduler provenance, and honest negative gates in one executable
workflow.
