\section{Conclusion}

Across \TestFamilyCount{} sealed families, the configured policy improves the
frozen generic reference by \ImprovementEstimate{} (family-clustered 95\% CI
[\ImprovementCILower{},~\ImprovementCIUpper{}]); \PositiveFamilyCount{} effects
are positive and \ZeroFamilyCount{} are zero, mainly replacing losses with
tick-cap draws. Yet its one-sided lower score margin against Supalosa is
\ChampionAbsoluteLower{}, so absolute superiority is not established.
Endpoint and component diagnostics are consistent with an infantry-rush policy
that fields more units but do not establish a mechanism. The contribution is a
provenance-bound, family-disjoint configuration/evaluation workflow and bounded
relative result---not a new environment, optimizer, or broad dominance claim.
