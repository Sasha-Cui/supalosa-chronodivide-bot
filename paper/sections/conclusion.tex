\section{Conclusion}

Across \TestFamilyCount{} sealed families, the configured policy improves the
frozen generic reference by \ImprovementEstimate{} (family-clustered 95\% CI
[\ImprovementCILower{},~\ImprovementCIUpper{}]); \PositiveFamilyCount{} effects
are positive and \ZeroFamilyCount{} are zero. Its one-sided lower score margin
against Supalosa is \ChampionAbsoluteLower{}, so the joint criterion fails and
absolute superiority is not established. The large, family-consistent gain
mainly reflects avoided losses, often via tick-cap draws. Diagnostics are
compatible with, but do not establish, an infantry-rush mechanism. The
contribution is a
provenance-bound, family-disjoint evaluation workflow---not a new environment,
optimizer, or dominance claim.

The reusable result is the evidence contract: instance lineage, random-stream
ownership, start balance, comparator isolation, and retry semantics must be
frozen together. Future prospective studies should preserve that contract
while adding independent opponents, faction pairings, supported theaters, and
action traces.
