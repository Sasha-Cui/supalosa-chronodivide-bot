\section{Conclusion}

We converted an exploratory Chrono Divide bot project into a deterministic,
family-disjoint, provenance-bound scripted-policy configuration study. Five
successive-halving searches and a common-seed championship selected one policy
from training families. Across \TestFamilyCount{} sealed test families, its score improves on
the shipped default by \ImprovementEstimate{} (family-clustered 95\% CI
[\ImprovementCILower{},~\ImprovementCIUpper{}]); \PositiveFamilyCount{} effects
are positive and \ZeroFamilyCount{} are zero, and the gain mainly converts
losses to tick-cap draws.

Yet its one-sided lower score margin against Supalosa is
\ChampionAbsoluteLower{}, so absolute superiority is not established. Endpoint
and component diagnostics suggest an infantry-rush policy that fields more units
but establish no mechanism after familywise correction. The contribution is a
reproducible configuration/evaluation workflow and bounded relative result---not
a new environment, optimizer, or broad game-AI dominance claim.
