\begin{abstract}
Scripted game agents are often tuned on a small set of maps, so reported gains
can conflate policy quality with map memorization, start position, simulator
randomness, or a contaminated opponent implementation. We present a
leakage-resistant evaluation protocol and reproducible configuration pipeline
for StrongBot in Chrono Divide, a browser reconstruction of
\emph{Command \& Conquer: Red Alert 2}.
The pipeline groups map revisions into families, screens simulator
compatibility without policy outcomes, isolates a pinned Supalosa opponent,
controls engine and participant randomness, swaps physical starts, seals
held-out outcomes, and binds every result to source, runtime, map, plan, and
scheduler commitments. Five run-distinct \OptimizerCandidateCount{}-policy
searches use deterministic
mutations and successive halving; a common-seed championship then selects one
coordinate-free generic policy using training families only. In a frozen
\ConfirmatoryGameCount{}-game evaluation over \TestFamilyCount{} sealed
families, the selected policy scores \ChampionScore{} versus \DefaultScore{}
for a frozen generic StrongBot reference. The equally family-weighted improvement is
\ImprovementEstimate{} (family-clustered 95\% CI
[\ImprovementCILower{},~\ImprovementCIUpper{}]); \PositiveFamilyCount{} family
effects are positive and \ZeroFamilyCount{} are zero. A separate prespecified
gate does not establish absolute
superiority: the one-sided 95\% lower bound for the selected policy's score
margin above 0.5 is \ChampionAbsoluteLower{}. The frozen joint two-gate
criterion therefore fails even though the relative-improvement gate passes.
Paired outcomes show that the
configured policy primarily replaces losses with survival or wins. Component
and terminal-state analyses are consistent with an infantry-rush policy that
converts more resources into fielded units, but neither endpoints nor the
multiplicity-controlled contrasts establish that mechanism. An identity-neutral
artifact regenerates all tables and figures. Together, the controls turn an
otherwise ambiguous tuning result into bounded, auditable evidence. The
comparison excludes the
deployed map-profile-enabled default and covers one opponent, matchup, and
supported map population.

\keywords{game artificial intelligence \and real-time strategy games \and
scripted agents \and algorithm configuration \and reproducible evaluation}
\end{abstract}
